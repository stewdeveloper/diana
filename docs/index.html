Diana

A free Discord bot that remembers every game your friend group has played together.

Companion apps track a player. Diana tracks a group - the three to eight people who queue together on a Tuesday night, in one private server. Add each other once, and Diana keeps the record: every match, every promotion, every streak, and a Sunday digest of who climbed and who did not.

Add Diana to your server

Free. No paid tier, no ads.

What it does

  • Match summaries. When a tracked player finishes a game, Diana posts it: result, champion, queue, role, KDA, damage, CS, vision - and the LP change if it was ranked.
  • Rank tracking. Promotions, demotions, new personal bests and win streaks, from a rank history Diana builds itself, match by match.
  • A Sunday digest. The group's week in one post - who climbed, who fell, who played the most games for the least reward.
  • Questions only a group can ask. /compare for head-to-head, /leaderboard for the server ladder, /champion for form on a pick, and duo win rates for when two of you queue together.
  • /livegame - is someone in a game right now? Champions, teams and bans, so the rest of you can spectate.

Getting started

  • Add Diana to your server with the button above.
  • /setchannel - pick where Diana posts.
  • /add name tag - start tracking a summoner. Repeat for each of you.
  • That's it. /help lists everything else.

Diana reads no message content and no member data. Everything it stores, and how to remove it, is described in the privacy policy.
